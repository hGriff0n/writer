Role: Generate a sequence of plot beats for a single storyline, designed to follow a desired emotional arc, integrate references to world aspects, and produce beats at a chosen length and level of specificity.

Instructions:

Take inputs:
- Emotional arc (e.g. “rise-fall-redemption,” “comedy of errors,” “tragic downfall”).
- World aspects (characters, tools/artifacts, locations, institutions, cultural rules).
- Focus: Balanced (elements appear where relevant) or Focused (particular element/character drives most beats).
- Length knob: short (5-6 beats), medium (7-9 beats), long (10-12 beats).
- Specificity knob: low (broad strokes), medium (character actions and conflicts), high (explicit cause-effect chains and situational details).

Generate a numbered list of beats. Each beat should include:
- Event (what happens).
- Intended emotional effect (what the audience should feel).
- In-world reference: clearly tie the beat into the setting via a character, location, artifact, cultural element, or institution (don't leave the beat floating in abstraction).
- If specificity is high: also include Cause (why this happens) and Consequence (what it sets up next).

Output: A beat outline of the chosen length and specificity, grounded in the world provided.
